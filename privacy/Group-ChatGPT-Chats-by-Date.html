Privacy Policy

Group ChatGPT Chats by Date

Last updated: August 18, 2026
Short version: This extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. Everything it does happens locally, inside your own browser.

What the extension does

Group ChatGPT Chats by Date adds collapsible date headers (Today, Yesterday, and specific dates) to the conversation sidebar on chatgpt.com, so your chat history is grouped by day instead of shown as one long list.

What data it accesses

To build these groups, the extension reads the conversation list that ChatGPT's own page already loads into your browser (chat titles and last-updated timestamps). This data:

  • Never leaves your browser
  • Is never sent to any server, including any server operated by the developer
  • Is not stored anywhere except transiently in memory while the page is open

What is stored, and where

The extension saves one small preference locally using Chrome's storage API: which date groups you've manually collapsed, and whether grouping is turned on. This is stored only on your own device (via chrome.storage.local/chrome.storage.sync, which is Google's own browser sync mechanism if you're signed into Chrome — not a server the developer controls or has access to).

Permissions used, and why

  • storage — save your collapse/expand preferences and the on/off toggle, locally on your device

The extension only runs on chatgpt.com. It has no access to any other site you visit.

Third parties

There are none. No analytics, no tracking pixels, no ad networks, no external API calls of any kind.
